A safe next chapter for every pet

Our mission

We connect animals in need with patient caregivers and loving homes across Minneapolis and Saint Paul. Through adoption, temporary foster care and community support, we help each pet take the next step toward a secure future.

Our community impact

In our sample year, 120 pets found homes, 45 foster households opened their doors and 80 volunteers contributed their time.

How you can help

  • Adopt a companion whose needs fit your household.
  • Offer a temporary foster home while a pet waits for adoption.
  • Volunteer with animal care, transport or community events.
  • Ask our team which supplies are currently most needed.
